# 🛡️ DocVault — Smart Personal Document & Asset Custodian
### *A Simple, Non-Technical Overview & Progress Report for Everyone*

---

## 🌟 What is DocVault?

**DocVault** is an intelligent, private digital locker designed for everyday people, families, and busy professionals. 

Instead of letting important papers and photos get lost in messy folders, phone galleries, or email threads, DocVault organizes your critical documents—like **bills, warranties, insurance policies, university certificates, and identity cards**—into clean, searchable records. 

Even better: you can **ask DocVault plain questions** in everyday English (like *"When does my car insurance expire?"* or *"What is my MacBook's serial number?"*) and get an immediate, accurate answer with the source document right in front of you.

---

## 🛑 The Problem DocVault Solves

Everyone experiences these frustrating moments:

1. **The "Digital Junk Drawer"**: You save receipts in Google Drive, photos on WhatsApp, and bills in email. When an appliance breaks, you spend 45 minutes searching for the warranty slip.
2. **Expired Warranties & Missed Deadlines**: Missing a car insurance renewal or home maintenance deadline often results in penalties, expensive out-of-pocket repairs, or lost coverage.
3. **Blurry & Unreadable Photos**: Snapping a quick picture of a receipt or contract with a smartphone often results in a blurry or tilted photo that you can't read months later when you actually need it.
4. **The Privacy Risk with Generic AI**: Uploading personal IDs, salary slips, or medical records to public AI tools (like standard ChatGPT) exposes your private information and trains public models on your personal data.

**DocVault fixes all of this in one secure place.**

### 1. 📸 Smart Upload with Anti-Blur Protection
* **What it does**: When you photograph a document or upload a scan, DocVault instantly analyzes the picture’s clarity.
* **Why it matters**: If your photo is blurry or out-of-focus, DocVault detects it immediately and politely asks you to retake it. This guarantees that every document in your vault remains crisp, readable, and legally valid whenever you need it.

### 2. 🗂️ Digital Asset Flashcards (Gadgets, Vehicles & Appliances)
* **What it does**: Keep neat digital cards for your physical possessions—like your **laptop, smartphone, car, refrigerator, or TV**.
* **Why it matters**: Each card holds your purchase date, serial number, insurance policy, and warranty expiration, and connects directly to your original purchase bill. You never have to rummage through desk drawers again.

### 3. 🤖 Ask AI Copilot (Your Personal Document Assistant)
* **What it does**: Chat naturally with your vault just like sending a message to a personal assistant.
* **Examples of questions you can ask**:
  * *"When does the warranty on my MacBook expire?"*
  * *"What was the total amount on my latest electricity bill?"*
  * *"What is the engine number and chassis number of my car?"*
  * *"What is the termination notice period in my service agreement?"*
* **Why it’s special**:
  * **Laser-Focused**: Answers you directly in the first sentence with key dates and numbers highlighted in **bold**.
  * **Multi-Page Precision**: Automatically finds the exact clause in 10+ page contracts and tells you the exact page number (e.g. *Page 5* or *Page 11*).
  * **In-Chat 1-Tap Preview**: Directly preview the original PDF contract or device flashcard right inside the chat window without leaving the conversation.
  * **Strict Entity Isolation**: Gadget queries never mix up with household utility bills.

### 4. ⏰ Automatic Deadline & Renewal Radar
* **What it does**: When you upload an insurance policy, registration card, or bill, DocVault automatically detects expiry dates and due dates.
* **Why it matters**: It organizes upcoming expirations into a visual timeline so you never miss a renewal deadline or get hit with late fees.

### 5. 🔒 Bank-Grade Security & Private Storage Mode
* **What it does**: Provides a **Two-Tier Security System**. You can mark ultra-sensitive documents (like your Passport, National ID, or Tax Returns) as **Storage-Only**.
* **Why it matters**: Storage-Only documents are locked behind a secondary **Vault PIN** and are **never** transmitted to any third-party AI service. Your most confidential identity documents remain 100% private.

### 6. 🤝 Safe & Expiring Share Links
* **What it does**: Share a specific document (e.g., sending a car registration to a mechanic or a tax receipt to an accountant) using a secure, temporary link.
* **Why it matters**: The recipient doesn't need an account, the link automatically expires when you want it to, and you can apply a security watermark to prevent unauthorized reuse.

## 🛠️ Technology Behind the Simplicity

Even though DocVault is simple and friendly for non-technical users, it is powered by production-grade engineering:

* **Frontend**: React 19, Vite, responsive Apple-inspired Titanium design system.
* **Backend**: Node.js & Express modular architecture with strict rate-limiting and security middleware.
* **Image Processing & Vision**: Sharp 2D Laplacian sharpness math engine + ONNX lightweight visual validation.
* **AI Intelligence**: Groq High-Speed LPU inference paired with local semantic vector embeddings.
* **Database & Storage**: MongoDB Atlas with binary internal encrypted storage.
